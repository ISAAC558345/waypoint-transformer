# Round-7 completion report

Round-7 is a non-learning Gate-B diagnosis and offline-data construction
round.  It generated `maritime_offline_dataset_v2` and did not train a
Waypoint Network, Transformer, PPO, HGNN, or any other neural network.

## Outputs

- Formal data/audit: `round_07/outputs/round07_formal_20260809_1740/`
- Figures: `round_07/figures/round07_formal_20260809_1740/` (20 PNG + 20 SVG)
- 60 scenarios, 720 complete legal episodes, and 214,080 v2 Waypoint samples;
  development and untouched holdout datasets are separate.
- The holdout has 12 scenarios / 144 episodes.  No scenario seed appears in
  more than one of train, validation, test, or holdout.

## Required answers

1. **Is v1 too easy?** For a Waypoint-vs-Direct-Goal claim, often yes:
   random/simple heuristics remain competitive and v1 did not sufficiently
   expose long-horizon trade-offs.
2. **Likely Gate-B cause:** the hindsight terminal goal directly contains
   terminal spatial information, alongside weak v1 low-quality/stitching
   coverage and H=30 multimodality.
3. **Does v1 have true stitching structure?** It has coarse overlap evidence,
   but not enough diverse bridge evidence to establish the intended structure.
4. **Does v2 increase overlap?** Yes: 74.61% cross-policy nearest-neighbour
   overlap and 1,641 P25 bridge candidates over 3,308 queries.
5. **Was low quality added lawfully?** Yes: 144/720 low-tier rollouts, all
   finite and action-safe, using causal legal behaviour generators.
6. **V2 quality ratio:** 40% high, 40% medium, 20% low.
7. **Difficulty ratio:** 20/20/20 easy/medium/hard scenarios.
8. **Is 13-D hindsight goal too strong/redundant?** Yes for deployment: it
   includes final UAV x/y; terminal-coordinate correlations are essentially
   one.  Padding/redundancy is explicitly audited rather than silently removed.
9. **Deployable goal candidate?** Yes, the pre-rollout three-field desired
   task goal, though it has no spatial destination.
10. **H=30 MSE averaging risk?** High.  It has local target variance 482.0
    and an opposite-direction rate 0.211 in the audited neighbourhoods.
11. **Recommended H candidates:** H=10 and H=20; no horizon is selected here.
12. **Long-term information beyond current action?** H=30 is weakly related
    to current action but not identical to it; the evidence is confounded by
    high multimodality, so the hierarchy is not established.
13. **Stress subset?** Yes, `stitching_stress_constructible=true`; it indexes
    real cross-episode candidate fragments and fabricates no transition.
14. **Development/holdout separated?** Yes, seed-level leakage count is zero.
15. **`retraining_data_ready`?** `true`; the gate records no blocking data
    integrity issue.  A later authorized round may only reconsider bounded
    Waypoint + Transformer retraining, never expand the environment directly.
