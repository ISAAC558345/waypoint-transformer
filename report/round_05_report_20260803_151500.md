## Offline findings

The WT validation selection correctly favors Hc=10: its three-seed test RMSE
is 17.1% lower than WT Hc=1. Time context also improves Direct-Goal and
State-only controls.

However, there is no stable evidence that predicted waypoints outperform the
controlled alternatives: Hc=10 WT is 0.0068 m worse than Direct-Goal on the
three-seed test mean, 0.0193 m better than State-only, and 0.0511 m worse than
the non-deployable Oracle diagnostic. Hc=10 WT improves over waypoint MLP by
17.9%, so temporal context is useful, but the waypoint-conditioning claim is
not established by this fixed pilot.

For the selected WT seed, raw test RMSE/MAE are 7.772/6.428 m (n=1,415), median
error 5.840 m, Q25/Q75 2.609/9.675 m, cosine similarity 0.381, and mean angular
error 57.775 degrees. Forty-eight predictions (3.39%) require radial
projection; projected action validity and target validity are both 1.0.

Selected WT policy groups show substantial heterogeneity: max_queue 11.011 m,
nearest_buoy 5.375 m, queue_rate_energy 6.332 m, random_valid 7.879 m. The
5-buoy subset (7.995 m) is worse than 3-buoy (7.280 m). Only 47/1,415 target
actions are below 3.333 m and the collection policy zero-action ratio is zero,
so a near-zero-action artifact does not explain the metric. The correlation
between frozen-waypoint error and selected-WT action error is weak (Pearson
`-0.0325`); it has no causal interpretation.

Validation-to-test shift remains material: model order among Hc=10 variants is
not identical across the two splits. No test result was used to alter the
already-frozen selection.

## Controlled interface and replay

Recorded-state replay covers selected representative episodes without advancing
an environment. The interface smoke uses a disposable real environment copy,
reproduces its recorded initial state exactly, executes five legal actions, and
recomputes state and frozen waypoint on every step. It intentionally does not
complete an episode, accumulate return, compare baselines, or claim a formal
closed-loop result.

## Limitations and next-stage readiness

`achieved_terminal_goal` is hindsight information. No online goal-generation
mechanism exists, so the selected WT is not claimed deployable. The fixed
dataset lacks low-quality trajectories, contains correlated within-episode
transitions, and has validation/test distribution shift. The results establish
offline action imitation plus a bounded engineering interface only; they do
not establish long-horizon policy improvement, communication return
improvement, safety, or formal closed-loop performance.

Round-5 is therefore complete but **not ready for formal closed-loop evaluation
without a separate, explicit authorization and a valid online goal protocol**.
No subsequent round is created automatically.
